## Recipient eligibility

- not already subscribe to Sales Navigator;
- not have used any LinkedIn free trial in the previous 12 months;
- not have another active LinkedIn Premium subscription, including Premium
  Career, Premium Business, or Recruiter Lite;
- not have redeemed the same two-month Sales Navigator referral offer before.

Eligibility is decided by LinkedIn. Before activation, the recipient should
read the checkout screen, including the price and renewal/cancellation terms.
Do not promise eligibility, a card-free activation, or automatic cancellation.

## Useful Sales Navigator Core benefits

- A dedicated B2B prospecting workspace for finding buyers, clients,
  decision-makers, partners, and target companies.
- More than 50 lead and company filters, including function, seniority,
  location, industry, experience, and years at a company.
- Saved searches and custom filters, plus alerts when relevant leads or
  accounts change.
- Saved leads and accounts, custom lists, notes, personas, and recommendations
  based on activity, mutual connections, and buyer interest.
- Real-time lead and account signals that create a natural reason to contact a
  prospect, such as a role change or recent activity.
- 50 InMail credits per month for contacting prospects outside the network.
- Sales Navigator includes the general Premium Business features as well.

## Whom to offer Sales Navigator

Offer it when the person's profile or their reply shows a genuine need for B2B
prospecting, for example:

- founder, co-founder, owner, entrepreneur, or managing director;
- sales, business development, account management, partnerships, growth, or
  go-to-market work;
- consultant, agency owner, or freelancer actively looking for clients;
- recruiter, business broker, or B2B service provider who searches for and
  contacts many potential customers or candidates;
- explicit mention of leads, pipeline, outbound, client acquisition, target
  accounts, partnerships, or decision-makers.

Do not infer a sales need merely from a senior title. When the profile is
ambiguous, ask what they want to use Premium for instead of pushing the more
expensive product.

## When Premium Business is the better offer

Prefer the separate Premium Business referral for someone mainly interested in
general networking, profile visibility, LinkedIn Learning, career development,
company research, or occasional messaging. Sales Navigator is the better fit
only when systematic lead/account discovery and outreach are central.

## Core plan boundaries and claims to avoid

- The gift is Sales Navigator **Core**, not Advanced or Advanced Plus.
- Do not promise TeamLink, Smart Links, team collaboration, shared team lists,
  or detailed team usage reporting; LinkedIn assigns those to Advanced tiers.
- Do not promise Account IQ, Lead IQ, or Message Assist; LinkedIn currently
  assigns those to Advanced and Advanced Plus.
- Do not promise CRM integration or lead/contact creation in a CRM; those are
  Advanced Plus features.
- Do not call InMail unlimited: LinkedIn currently states 50 credits per month.
- Do not present marketing averages as a guaranteed personal outcome.
