## What Liubov can currently share

- 10 available referral links were shown on the authenticated page.
- Each eligible recipient can receive two free months of Premium Business.
- A referral is generated after clicking `Empfehlungslink teilen`.
- Each generated referral can be redeemed once.
- A generated referral expires after two weeks.
- If it expires unused, LinkedIn says it can be sent again.
- Referral links refresh 12 months after their original grant date.
- Liubov's Sales Navigator subscription is why the shareable plan is Premium
  Business. LinkedIn's FAQ says Sales Navigator members can share Business;
  Premium Career or Premium Business members can share Career.

## Recipient eligibility and billing caveats

- Eligibility is decided by LinkedIn, not by Liubov.
- A current Premium member may be ineligible.
- Someone who previously used a free Premium trial may be ineligible.
- An expired referral cannot be redeemed.
- LinkedIn's general trial help says a valid payment card is required.
- Unless canceled before the trial ends, the subscription automatically
  converts to a paid plan.
- LinkedIn says the Premium plan cannot be switched during the trial.
- Do not promise eligibility, card-free activation, or automatic cancellation.

## Useful Premium Business benefits

### Networking and research

- Up to 15 InMail credits per month for messaging people outside the network.
- Unlimited search for people and companies.
- Advanced search and unrestricted viewing beyond the immediate network.
- Suggestions for warm introductions through existing connections.
- Company insights such as growth, hiring activity, and employee trends.

### Profile visibility and credibility

- Full profile-viewer information and analytics; current LinkedIn help describes
  up to 365 days of viewer data, subject to visitors' privacy settings.
- Viewer filters, weekly trends, and breakdowns by company, industry, and
  location.
- A custom call-to-action button on the profile and other LinkedIn surfaces.
- Featured profile content for credentials, awards, and important experience.
- A dynamic profile cover with up to five images.
- AI-assisted suggestions for improving the profile.
- Additional Premium placements intended to improve visibility.

### Learning

- Full LinkedIn Learning access. The current product page says more than 25,000
  courses; the referral page itself displayed more than 16,000, so use the
  conservative phrase "тысячи курсов" in casual messages.
- AI-powered learning coaching and content recommendations.

## Claims to avoid

- Premium Business does not include Sales Navigator.
- Do not call InMail unlimited: the detailed Business page specifies 15 monthly
  credits.
- Do not guarantee that private-mode profile visitors will be identified.
- Do not promise that every recipient can redeem the trial.
- Do not hide the payment-card and automatic-renewal caveats.
- Do not present LinkedIn's marketing averages as an individual guarantee.
